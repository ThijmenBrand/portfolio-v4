calsync
  Keep your calendar subscriptions up to date in the background, so they are
  fresh when you open the Calendar app and the top bar's upnext is never
  showing yesterday's events. It has no window and no icon. It detaches from
  the shell, so closing the terminal keeps it.

  It starts at login on its own, from /etc/autostart; remove its line there
  to stop that. Every minute it syncs the subscriptions that are due, by
  the same schedule the Calendar app uses (the feed's own refresh interval,
  with a longer wait after failures), and any that have no cached copy yet.

  It reads the same subscription list and writes the same cache as the
  Calendar app. Subscribe, rename and remove calendars in the app (see
  `man calendar`); stop calsync with `kill` or from the Task Manager.

todo
  Things to do, some on a day and some at a time of day. Tick one off and
  it plays a short chime and moves to Completed; the speaker button in the
  corner turns the chime off.

  Every todo with a date also appears in Calendar: at its time of day when
  it has one, as an all-day item when it doesn't. Ticking it off there
  ticks it off here, and the other way round.

  The list lives in /mnt/cloud/todo/todos.json where that mount will have
  you -- in which case every browser you sign in to shows the same list --
  and in ~/.config/todo.json otherwise. The app says which one you have.

  Keys: N new todo, Up/Down move, Space done, E edit, Delete remove.

  See also: calendar.

todo
  Things to do. Each can have a day you plan to do it (and a time, and how
  long it takes), a deadline it has to be done by, both, or neither. Tick
  one off and it plays a short chime and moves to Completed; the speaker
  button in the corner turns the chime off.

  The list is ordered by when you plan to do things: Today, Tomorrow,
  Upcoming. A plan you didn't get to carries over to Today. The deadline
  sits beside each todo: grey while there's time, amber within three days,
  red once it has passed. Anything past its deadline goes to the top; a
  deadline with no plan waits under "Needs a plan". The editor warns when
  a plan ends after its deadline, and offers to move it.

  Every todo with a plan or a deadline also appears in Calendar. Ticking it
  off there ticks it off here, and the other way round.

  The list lives in /mnt/cloud/todo/todos.json where that mount will have
  you -- in which case every browser you sign in to shows the same list --
  and in ~/.config/todo.json otherwise. The app says which one you have.

  Keys: N new todo, Up/Down move, Space done, E edit, T do it today,
  P plan, D deadline, Delete remove.

  From anywhere, Cmd+K (Ctrl+K off a Mac) opens Spotlight: "New todo"
  takes the whens from the line itself -- "call mum fri 14:00 for 30m",
  "essay due 3/11 17:00", "15:00 dentist" (a time alone is today) -- and
  without any it has no plan. Words in "quotes" are left alone. Typing part
  of an open todo's title there offers to mark it done. The same program
  runs in a terminal: todo.commands new "buy milk tomorrow".

  See also: calendar.

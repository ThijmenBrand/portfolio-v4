bundle <entry.js> [-o out.app.js] [--name NAME] [--icon FILE] [--install]
  Pack <entry.js> and every module it imports into one .app.js file that
  runs on its own: double-click it, download it, share it.
  Name, icon, version and window size come from app.json next to the entry:
    {"name": "Hello", "icon": "icon.svg", "version": "1.0", "window": {"width": 480, "height": 320}}
  --install also copies it into your apps folder, so it shows in the app menu.
  Writing apps: see README.md on the Desktop.

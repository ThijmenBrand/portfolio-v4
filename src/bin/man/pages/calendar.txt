calendar [file.ics]
  Your calendars, in a month, week, day or agenda view: the ones you make
  yourself, which you can write in, and the ones you subscribe to, which are
  read-only.

  Your own calendars are listed first in the sidebar; "+ New calendar" makes
  one. Make an event with the + button (or N), by double-clicking a day or
  an hour slot, or by dragging down the hour grid. Click an event to edit or
  delete it. A repeating event is edited as a whole series. Each calendar is
  one .ics file, in /mnt/cloud/calendar/mine/ where that mount will have you
  and in ~/.config/calendar/mine/ otherwise. A subscribed event can be copied
  into one of your calendars with "Copy to my calendar", and edited there.

  Two kinds of subscription, both added from the sidebar:
    link   an https .ics address. webcal:// links are accepted too.
    file   an .ics file from this machine, chosen with the file picker.
  Given an .ics path on the command line, that file is imported on startup.

  Each calendar's last body is cached under ~/.config/calendar/, so the views
  draw instantly on launch and keep working with no network. Subscriptions
  themselves live in ~/.config/calendar.json, and in cloud storage as
  /mnt/cloud/calendar/subscriptions.json where that mount will have you -- in
  which case every browser you sign in to shows the same list. The sidebar
  says which of the two you are looking at.

  Dated todos from the Todo app show up too: at the time you planned them
  (as long as you planned them for) if they have one, as all-day items if
  not. A plan whose day went by stays there, dashed, and is carried over to
  today. Tick the box to mark one done.

  An open todo's deadline shows as a flag: a line across its day at its
  time, or an all-day chip. Grey while there's time, amber within three
  days, red once it has passed. Hover a todo to light up its deadline, and
  the other way round. The Todos and Deadlines switches in the sidebar hide
  them.

  Keys: N new event, T today, arrows move, Enter open the focused day,
  M/W/D/A switch view, R refresh, Esc close. Event chips are reachable by
  pointer; the grid itself takes one tab stop.

  See also: upnext, for the same calendars in the top bar.

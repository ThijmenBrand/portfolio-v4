wm [list]
wm <id> left|right|top-left|top-right|bottom-left|bottom-right|maximize|restore
  List windows (* marks the focused one), or snap one into half or a quarter
  of the screen, maximize it, or restore its floating size.
  Reaches every window of a program running as you, like kill.
  wm 3 left; wm 4 right. Dragging a window to a screen edge or corner does the
  same, and so does Ctrl+Alt+Arrow on the focused window.

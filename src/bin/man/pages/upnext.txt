upnext
  Show the next event from your subscribed calendars in the top bar, like a
  macOS menu bar extra. It detaches from the shell, so closing the terminal
  keeps it. Click the icon for the next fortnight's events, or to open the
  Calendar app.

  Read-only: it shows what Calendar has already fetched and never syncs on
  its own. Subscribe to calendars in the Calendar app (see `man calendar`).
